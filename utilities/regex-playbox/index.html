Regex Cheatsheet

Character Classes

  • . Any character except newline
  • \w Word character [a-zA-Z0-9_]
  • \d Digit [0-9]
  • \s Whitespace
  • \W Not a word
  • \D Not a digit
  • \S Not whitespace

Anchors

  • ^ Start of string
  • $ End of string
  • \b Word boundary

Quantifiers

  • * 0 or more
  • + 1 or more
  • ? 0 or 1
  • {3} Exactly 3
  • {3,} 3 or more
  • {3,5} 3, 4, or 5

Groups & Sets

  • [abc] a, b, or c
  • [^abc] Not a, b, or c
  • [a-z] Characters a to z
  • (xyz) Capture group
  • (?:xyz) Non-capturing group
  • x|y x or y

Common Flags

  • g Global (don't stop at first match)
  • i Case insensitive
  • m Multiline (impacts ^ and $)
