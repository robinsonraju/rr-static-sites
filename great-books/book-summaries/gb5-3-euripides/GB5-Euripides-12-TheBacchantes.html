The Bacchantes The Revels of Blood

Perhaps the most terrifying of all tragedies. The young king Pentheus tries to suppress the new religion of Dionysus, only to be seduced and destroyed by the very god he denies.

📈 The Struggle for Thebes

Comparing Dionysus' effortless manipulation against Pentheus' collapsing order.

Dionysus's Path: Starts as a mysterious 'stranger' in bonds, ends as an all-powerful, vengeful god atop the ruins of a family.
Pentheus's Path: Starts as a rigid, angry king, is slowly driven mad/infatuated with the god, and ends as a sacrifice to his own mother.
