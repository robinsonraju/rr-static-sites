Hecuba The Dog of Vengeance

A dark, brutal play about the transformation of a victim into a monster. Hecuba, having lost everything at Troy, finds that the only thing left to her is a savage, bloody revenge.

📈 The Descent into Vengeance

Comparing Hecuba's loss of humanity with Polymestor's loss of everything.

Hecuba's Path: Starts as a mourning queen, loses her last daughter and son, and becomes a systematic, cold-blooded executioner.
Polymestor's Path: Starts as a greedy, confident traitor, and ends blinded, childless, and howling like a beast.

🔍 Deep-Dive: The End of Hospitality

The play focuses on the violation of Xenia (hospitality) and its horrific consequences.

The Blinding

Hecuba's choice to blind Polymestor instead of killing him is a calculated move to ensure he feels the darkness he has brought into her life.

The Justice

A literal 'eye for an eye' in a world where gods are silent.
