THE ODYSSEY

Book IX: I am Noman

"I am Ulysses, Son of Laertes"

"Renowned among mankind for all manner of subtlety... there is nothing dearer to a man than his own country and his parents."

The Name

By revealing his name, Ulysses ends his anonymity but seals his fate with Polyphemus's father.

The Mission

He begins the "Apologoi"—the flashback narrative of his nine-year wandering.

The Casualty Count

Ulysses' journey is marked by the gradual erosion of his fleet. At Ismarus, the men's greed costs lives; at the Lotus island, they lose their will to return.

Ismarus (Cicones)

The men stayed to drink and feast against Ulysses' orders. Result: Six men per ship killed in a counter-attack.

The Lotus-Eaters

"Those who ate of it left off caring about home." Ulysses had to drag them back to the ships by force.

Escape from the Cave

A step-by-step interactive log of the stratagem used to blind Polyphemus and escape his reach.

1. The Maron Wine

Ulysses brings a skin of black wine so potent it requires 20 parts water. He gives it to the Cyclops "neat" to induce a heavy stupor.

2. The "Noman" Logic

When asked his name, Ulysses says "Noman." This prevents the other Cyclopes from helping when they hear: "Noman is killing me by fraud!"

3. The Olive Stake

A six-foot beam of green olive wood is sharpened, charred, and driven into the Cyclops' single eye while he sleeps.

4. Under the Bellies

The men are bound in threes under the fat sheep. Ulysses clings to the fleece of the lead ram. Polyphemus feels the backs of the sheep, never the undersides.

The Hubris of the Hero

Safely at sea, Ulysses cannot resist shouting his true name. Polyphemus then prays to Neptune with a specific four-part curse:

  • ⚔️ Grant that Ulysses never reach his home alive.
  • ⚔️ Or, if he must return, let it be late and in sore plight.
  • ⚔️ Let him reach home after losing all his men.
  • ⚔️ Let him reach home in another man's ship and find trouble.

"And Neptune heard his prayer."
