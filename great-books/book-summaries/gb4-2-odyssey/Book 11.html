The Prophecy of Tiresias
1

The Thrinacian Test

Restrain yourself and your companions from harming the cattle of the Sun-god Hyperion. If you harm them, your ship and men will perish.

2

The Return to Ithaca

You will find high-handed people devouring your substance and paying court to your wife. You must take your revenge on these suitors.

3

The Inland Journey

Carry an oar inland until someone mistakes it for a winnowing shovel. Fix it there and sacrifice to Neptune.

4

A Peaceful End

Death shall come to you gently from the sea when you are full of years and peace of mind.

The Tortured Souls

Tantalus

Stands in a lake that recedes when he stoops to drink. Fruit branches pull away when he reaches for food. Eternal hunger and thirst.

Sisyphus

Must roll a prodigious stone to the top of a hill, only for it to thunder back down just as it reaches the peak. Eternal labor.

Tityus

Stretched over nine acres of ground while two vultures eternally peck and dig at his liver. Punishment for violating Leto.
