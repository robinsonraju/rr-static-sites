The Offering

The Pylians are gathered on the shore, offering black bulls to Neptune. This ritual establishes the profound piety of Nestor's kingdom.

The Laws of Xenia

How Nestor treats the "unknown" travelers.

1

The Welcome

Before asking their names, Nestor provides food, wine, and a place by the fire. "Now that our guests have done their dinner, it will be best to ask them who they are."

2

The Shared Ritual

Pisistratus gives the cup to Minerva (Mentor) first because she is older, showing respect for age and the gods.

3

The Provision

Nestor refuses to let Telemachus sleep on the ship, providing rugs, cloaks, and eventually a chariot with his own son to Sparta.
