A Covenant of Peace

I. FORGIVENESS: The people of Ithaca shall forgive and forget the massacre of their sons and brothers.

II. SOVEREIGNTY: Ulysses, son of Laertes, shall continue to rule as the rightful King of Ithaca.

III. PLENTY: Peace and plenty shall reign throughout the land, as decreed by the Son of Saturn.

IV. DIVINE MANDATE: No further warful strife shall be permitted, lest the wrath of Jove be kindled.
