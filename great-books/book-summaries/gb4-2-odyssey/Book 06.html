Civic Planning of the Far West

The Market Place

Paved with large stones, featuring a temple of Neptune in the middle. Centers of shipping gear and cable commerce.

The Harbour

Narrow entrance with ships drawn up on either side. Every citizen has a private place for their vessel.

The Palace

Unmatched in the town. Surrounded by an outer court and a high wall. Home to Alcinous and Arete.

The Grove

Dedicated to Minerva, featuring a well and a meadow. Alcinous's rich garden ground is nearby.
