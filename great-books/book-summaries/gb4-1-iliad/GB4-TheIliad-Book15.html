Book XV witnesses the absolute assertion of Zeus's will. As the king of gods awakens to find his plans thwarted by Neptune and Juno, he restores the Trojan momentum with terrifying force. Hector, healed by Apollo, leads a desperate charge to fire the Greek fleet, while Ajax stands as the last bastion of Achaean hope.
