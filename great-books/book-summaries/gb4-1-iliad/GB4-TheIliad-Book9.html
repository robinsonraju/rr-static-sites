The Embassy to Achilles

"Him do I hate even as the gates of hell who says one thing while he hides another in his heart."

Book IX is the intellectual and rhetorical heart of the Iliad. Facing total defeat, Agamemnon sends a desperate delegation to Achilles, offering immense treasures for his return. The resulting debate explores the limits of reward, the nature of honor, and the irreconcilable rift between the hero and the king.
