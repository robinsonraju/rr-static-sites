The Shield of Achilles

"An iron clank rose through the void air to the brazen vault of heaven."

Book XVIII is a masterpiece of contrast: the devastating, earth-shaking grief of Achilles meets the divine, orderly creation of Hephaestus. As Achilles accepts his mortal fate to avenge Patroclus, the gods forge for him a shield that contains the entire world—a cosmic reminder of the life he is about to leave behind for the sake of eternal glory.
